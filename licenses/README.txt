The extraction worker bundles libarchive-wasm 1.2.0 (MIT) and its WebAssembly
decoder. Upstream build references: https://github.com/ofk/libarchive-wasm
and https://github.com/ofk/libarchive-wasm/blob/main/lib/Dockerfile

Notices in this directory cover libarchive-wasm, libarchive 3.7.7, zlib 1.3.1,
bzip2 1.0.8, XZ/liblzma 5.6.4, OpenSSL 3.4.1, and Emscripten 4.0.5.
They are copied into the extension release alongside the bundled decoder.
